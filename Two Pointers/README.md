# Two Pointers

## 📌 Overview
The Two Pointers technique involves iterating with two pointers over a sequence (e.g., array or string) from opposite ends or at different speeds to optimize time complexity from \(O(N^2)\) to \(O(N)\).

## 🛠️ Common Patterns & Techniques
- Opposite Direction Pointers (Sorted array pair search, Palindrome check)
- Same Direction Pointers (Fast & Slow pointers, Remove duplicates in-place)
- Merging two sorted lists/arrays
